## Abstract

Inventory mismanagement is the single largest operational failure point in Indian Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). Despite operating complex multi-SKU supply chains, most Indian SMEs continue to monitor inventory manually — through spreadsheets, weekly reviews, and reactive decision-making that arrives only after damage is already done. The consequences compound: overstocking ties up working capital in slow-moving goods, understocking kills customer orders and damages supplier relationships, dead stock occupies warehouse space with zero return, and late reordering causes lead-time disruptions that cascade across operations. Collectively, these failures drive an estimated **6–8% annual revenue loss** across Indian SMEs, making inventory failure the primary contributor to operational inefficiency in the sector.

**SupplyMind** addresses this problem by introducing an autonomous AI intelligence layer designed to sit on top of an existing Supply Chain Management (SCM) system — not replace it. Rather than asking business owners to monitor dashboards and compute reorder quantities manually, SupplyMind detects risks, computes decisions, and delivers explainable alerts *before* the business owner opens a screen.

The framework is built around **three autonomous engines**, each owning a distinct inventory risk domain:

- **Stockout Prevention Engine** — Applies Poisson distribution to compute per-SKU stockout probability in real time. When Days of Supply falls below 7, the engine autonomously fires a reorder recommendation with supplier, quantity, and cost breakdown.

- **Dead Stock Detection Engine** — Continuously identifies low-turnover SKUs with high carrying cost burdens. Before losses compound, it recommends markdown or return-to-supplier action, protecting warehouse capacity and working capital simultaneously.

- **Reorder Optimization Engine** — Computes Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) per SKU, factoring in supplier lead time variance. The output is a specific, actionable reorder package: quantity, timing window, and supplier selection — eliminating the guesswork from procurement.

The system is orchestrated through a **LangGraph agent graph**, with **Claude API** serving as the reasoning core for generating human-readable, explainable alerts. A Python + Pandas KPI computation engine tracks **8 continuous inventory health metrics** across all SKUs. Data is persisted in **PostgreSQL** with **Redis** providing event streaming for real-time threshold monitoring. The backend is exposed through a **FastAPI** layer, consumed by a **React + Recharts** dashboard that functions as a human oversight panel with a full agent audit trail.

A sample autonomous alert generated by the system:

> *"SKU Turmeric 500g — 4 days of supply remaining. Stockout probability: 94%. Reorder 1,200 units from Supplier B today. Estimated cost ₹48,000. Dead stock: SKU Pepper 250g at 0.8× turnover — recommend 15% markdown."*

Alerts are delivered via **Twilio WhatsApp** for immediate mobile reach, ensuring the business owner is informed at the moment of risk — not after it becomes a crisis.

SupplyMind integrates with any existing SCM system through a **REST API or CSV export interface**, pulling SKU stock levels, reorder points, and supplier lead times without requiring migration or re-platforming. The full system is deployed on **Azure with Docker**, enabling production-grade reliability and horizontal scalability.

Validation has been conducted across **three sectors** — manufacturing, retail distribution, and agri-processing SMEs — demonstrating cross-domain applicability of the agentic inventory layer. The system is targeted for publication in **IEEE Access** as the first agentic AI inventory layer purpose-built for Indian SME supply chain management.

Post-validation, the framework is designed to expand into **Procurement** and **Fulfillment** domains, extending autonomous intelligence beyond inventory into adjacent supply chain functions.

This work aligns with **SDG 9** (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), **SDG 12** (Responsible Consumption and Production), and **SDG 2** (Zero Hunger) — recognizing that efficient food and goods supply chains are foundational to economic equity and resource sustainability.

## Integration

SupplyMind connects to an existing SCM system as an intelligent layer — not a replacement.

1. Connects via **REST API or CSV export** from existing SCM
2. Pulls **SKU levels, reorder points, and supplier lead times**
3. KPI engine computes **8 inventory metrics** continuously
4. Agent pipeline fires **autonomously** on threshold breach
5. Alert delivered via **WhatsApp** and **React dashboard**
